## Overview
*Anne with an E* is a Canadian period drama television series loosely adapted from Lucy Maud Montgomery's 1908 classic work of children's literature, Anne of Green Gables. 

The series tackles a range of issues like helping orphans, child abandonment, psychological trauma, social issues such as conformity, gender inequality, racism, religion, homosexuality, bullying, and freedom of speech.

## Sypnosis

The show follows the adventures of Anne Shirley, young orphan who is seeking love, acceptance and her place in the worl after enduring an abusive childhood in orphanages and the homes of strangers. In the late 1890s, Anne is mistakenly sent to live with aging siblings, Marilla and Matthew Cuthbert, who live on Prince Edward Island. Anne, who proves to be uniquely spirited, imaginative and smart, transforms the lives of Marilla, Matthew and everyone else in their small town.

## Famous Quotes

> “I am so glad to be alive in this world.” – Anne Shirley

This simple yet profound statement reminds us to cherish our existence. Life is full of ups and downs, yet finding joy in the mere fact that we are alive is essential. Anne’s appreciation for life encourages us to embrace all its facets, even the challenges, which help us grow.

> “Every day may not be good, but there is something good in every day.” – Anne Shirley

This quote highlights the importance of finding positivity even during tough times. Just like Anne teaches us, it is vital to focus on the little joys that can brighten our days. By accepting that not every moment is perfect, we can cultivate resilience and gratitude.

> “Kindred spirits are not so scarce as true love.” – Anne Shirley

This quote places value on friendships that resonate deeply with us. Just like Anne, we may find connections that feel rare and unique. Kindred spirits are those who understand us on a soul level and can inspire beautiful bonds.
